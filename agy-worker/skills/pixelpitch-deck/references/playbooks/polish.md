# Playbook: polish

For: the final lift on a structurally sound deck. No structural change —
no new plates, no re-specimen, no aid type swaps.

## Steps (verbatim)

1. Read the principles index and the current renders.
2. Title pass: every title is an assertion a reader could argue with; the
   titles read top-to-bottom as the whole argument. Strengthen the weak
   ones (specific, falsifiable, no "and").
3. So-what pass: each slide's implication is stated on the slide, not
   implied under a chart.
4. Whitespace and weight: one focal point per slide; dramatic (not timid)
   hierarchy; generous margins; nothing cramped at the bottom edge.
5. Accent discipline: exactly one accent per slide marking the point; remove
   every decorative use of colour.
6. Copy pass: em/en-dashes out of titles; concrete specifics; numbers with
   referents; cut every word the slide survives without.
7. Reconvert, gate, and personally inspect every changed render.
8. Report: per slide, what changed and what deliberately did not.

## Rules

- Polish is subtraction plus emphasis, never addition of new elements.
- If polish keeps wanting to change structure, stop — that is the repair or
  arena playbook, not this one.
